About us

Founded by graduates who lived the IB

IB Bridge is a mentoring and academic support initiative founded by recent IB Diploma graduates who are now in their first year of university. Having completed the IB programme ourselves, we understand its academic intensity, structure, and expectations — not from theory, but from lived experience.

Today, our tutors are pursuing degrees aligned with the subjects they excelled in during the IB. This means students are guided by individuals who have not only achieved strong results, but are continuing to build expertise in those fields at university level.

We bridge the gap between high school and higher education — with clarity, structure, and perspective.

IB Bridge was founded by Sofia, a 40-point IB graduate, who created the initiative and leads it as CEO.
