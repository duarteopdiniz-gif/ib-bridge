About us

Founded by graduates who lived the IB

IB Bridge is a mentoring and academic support initiative founded by recent IB Diploma graduates who are now at university.

Having lived the IB first-hand lets us understand its demands, its structure, and everything it involves — not just in theory, but from our own experience.

Today, our tutors study degrees related to the subjects they excelled in during the IB. This way, our students get support from people who not only achieved excellent results, but keep developing in those same fields at university level.

We build a bridge between school and university — with clarity, structure, and perspective.

IB Bridge was created by Sofia, a 40-point IB graduate, who currently leads the initiative and coordinates our team of tutors.
